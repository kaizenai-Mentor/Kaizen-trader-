Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KAIZEN ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Platform at kaizen-trader.onrender.com.

KAIZEN handles sensitive personal information, including your trading behavior and, where you choose to share it, information about your psychological state related to trading. We take this responsibility seriously and aim to be fully transparent about our data practices.

02 Information We Collect

Account Information
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
  • Google account information, if you register or log in via Google OAuth (name, email, profile ID)
Trading Profile Information
  • Your stated trading style, including risk per trade, daily drawdown limits, entry and exit rules, trading edge/strategy, emotional triggers, and markets traded
  • This information is provided voluntarily during onboarding and can be updated at any time
Journal Entries
  • The free-text descriptions of your trading sessions that you submit
  • Whether you indicated rule compliance for each session
  • Detected asset, timeframe, and outcome information
Psychology Session Content
  • Messages you send during Psychology Sessions, which may include sensitive information about your emotional state, financial pressures, fears, or mindset related to trading
Wallet Data
  • If you connect a wallet: the wallet address (stored encrypted)
  • Wallet verification nonces (stored only as cryptographic hashes)
  • Versioned consent records for each wallet connection
  • Wallet addresses are private by default and are never displayed publicly unless you explicitly choose to publish them
Usage Data
  • Login times and session activity
  • Pages and features accessed within the Platform
  • KAIZEN Score history and streak data
On-Chain Data
  • If you connect a Zero Authority DAO account, your ZA user ID and associated reputation data
  • A one-way cryptographic hash of your internal user ID may be recorded on the Mantle blockchain, along with earned credentials and milestones, all in anonymized form

03 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate AI-powered feedback on your journal entries and psychology sessions
  • Calculate and display your KAIZEN Score
  • Detect behavioral patterns across your sessions (such as recurring FOMO or rule violations) to provide more relevant feedback over time
  • Send you account-related emails, including welcome emails and, where enabled, weekly summary emails
  • Show your progress over time and, where you choose to participate, community challenge results
  • Maintain and improve the Platform
  • Investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service

04 How Your Data Is Processed by Third Parties

To provide KAIZEN's core functionality, certain data is shared with third-party service providers:

Anthropic: Your journal entries and psychology session messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate AI feedback. Anthropic processes this text according to their own data handling practices. We do not control how Anthropic's underlying infrastructure processes this data beyond generating the response returned to KAIZEN.

MongoDB Atlas: All account data, journal entries, and psychology session content are stored in MongoDB Atlas, a cloud database service. Data is stored in a database we control and is not accessible to MongoDB Atlas employees for any purpose other than infrastructure operation.

Google: If you use Google OAuth to register or log in, Google provides us with basic profile information (name, email, profile ID) as authorized by you during the Google sign-in process.

Render: Our application is hosted on Render's infrastructure. Usage data necessary to operate the service may be processed by Render.

Resend: Used to send transactional emails such as welcome messages and weekly summaries. Your email address is shared with Resend solely for this purpose.

Wallet providers: Wallet connection and verification happen through your wallet's own software. Signing happens on your device — KAIZEN never receives, transmits, or stores your seed phrase or private keys. We receive only your public wallet address and the signature needed to verify it.

Zero Authority DAO: If you choose to connect your ZA account, your ZA user ID is used to retrieve your public reputation data from the ZA DAO platform. We do not share your KAIZEN journal content or psychology session content with Zero Authority DAO.

Mantle Blockchain: A cryptographic hash of your user ID (not your actual identity, email, or any personal information) may be recorded on the Mantle blockchain along with earned credentials and milestones, so your progress can be independently verified. These records currently run on a public testnet. Raw journal text, psychology session content, and any personally identifiable information are never written to any blockchain.

05 Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plain text
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Account data is stored in MongoDB Atlas with access restricted to authorized systems
  • Connected wallet addresses are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); lookups use keyed HMAC fingerprints rather than raw addresses; verification nonces are stored only as SHA-256 hashes
  • We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure

06 Data Retention

  • Journal entries and AI-generated responses ("Memories") are retained indefinitely to allow KAIZEN to track your behavioral patterns over time and provide increasingly personalized feedback
  • If you delete your account, your personal account data, journal entries, and psychology session content will be permanently removed from our active database
  • Hashed, anonymized data already recorded on the Mantle blockchain cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain technology. However, this data contains no personally identifiable information and cannot be linked back to you without access to our internal database

07 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information via your account settings
  • Delete your account and associated personal data at any time using the Delete Account feature
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing, where applicable
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format by contacting us

KAIZEN is a Nigerian business serving a global audience. Personal data is processed in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, in addition to the rights described above.

08 Children's Privacy

KAIZEN is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will take steps to delete their account and associated data.

09 Cookies and Session Data

KAIZEN uses a single session cookie to keep you logged in, and stores your theme preference (dark or light) locally on your device. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

10 International Data Transfers

KAIZEN's infrastructure, including MongoDB Atlas and hosting via Render, may process and store data in regions outside your country of residence. By using KAIZEN, you consent to this transfer and processing of your data.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as KAIZEN evolves. Material changes will be communicated to users where reasonably possible. Continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

12 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us via X at @kaizenaibot or through the Support page on the Platform.

The Mission

About KAIZEN

Built by a trader, for traders. KAIZEN exists because the gap between knowing your rules and following them is where most accounts go to die.

The Problem
Most retail traders lose money.

Not because of bad strategies. Not because of the market. Because of behavioral failures — FOMO entries, revenge trades, rule violations under pressure, overconfidence after wins. The strategy works. The execution does not.

The Solution

Turn the invisible struggle into measurable improvement.

KAIZEN is a trader improvement platform. You plan your trades, journal your sessions, and reflect on what happened. The AI analyzes every entry against your own stated rules and your full history — so every response is specific to your behavior, your patterns, your triggers, your blind spots. Not a template. Not generic advice.

The result is the KAIZEN Score: one number, five dimensions — Process, Risk, Execution, Behavior, Learning & Consistency. It measures how you trade, not what you made, because improvement is the one thing you can actually control.

The Score, Explained

How is this scored?

The KAIZEN Score is computed by a fixed formula from your sessions — never invented by the AI, never set by hand. It measures how you traded, not what you made. Every number shows its WHY, and when there isn't enough evidence yet, a dimension says Building instead of guessing.

Process

Do you do what you said you would? Planning before the session, and trading inside your own written rules.

Risk

How responsibly you manage it — predefined risk, sizing, stops, and the daily limits you set for yourself.

Execution

How closely the actual trade matched the plan you wrote beforehand — entries, exits, and the moments in between.

Behavior

The patterns behind your decisions — the impulses, the hesitations, the things that repeat when nobody is watching.

Learning & Consistency

Whether experience is actually turning into improvement — real reflections, corrected mistakes, steadiness over time.

What Makes KAIZEN Different
Behavioral AI, Not Signals

KAIZEN never tells you what to trade. It tells you whether you followed your own process and identifies exactly where and why your discipline breaks down across sessions. The intelligence grows with every entry you log.

Psychology, Not Just Trade Analysis

A dedicated Psychology Session space lets you talk to KAIZEN AI about your mindset, fear of pulling the trigger, financial pressure, or self-doubt — not tied to any specific trade. Discipline failures are often rooted in psychology that never shows up in a trade log.

# KAIZEN — Master Vision & Roadmap (LOCKED)

**Source:** the owner's master vision document, 15 Sep 2026 — reviewed, reconciled
against the built product, and locked as the strategic reference before further
work. Product-level frozen specs remain in `config/`; this doc is the layer above
them: what KAIZEN is, where it goes, and what we deliberately do NOT build yet.

**Current position (confirmed by this doc):** Heavy **Stage 1** (Trader
Improvement — essentially complete) + early **Stage 2** (Evidence).

---

## 1. Why KAIZEN exists

The problem was never only "I don't have a strategy." A trader can have a
working strategy and still fail. The real problems:

- inconsistency
- lack of structured study
- poor accountability
- emotional decision-making
- breaking one's own rules
- repeating the same mistakes
- inadequate reflection
- poor risk discipline
- failing to turn experience into learning

**The fundamental belief:**

> A trader doesn't improve simply by trading more. A trader improves by
> deliberately learning from how they trade.

KAIZEN = continuous improvement. KAIZEN does not promise "use us and become
profitable." It helps the trader build the behaviors, systems and evidence
that contribute to becoming a better trader.

## 2. The North Star

> KAIZEN is an operating system for trader development — helping traders
> build better processes, understand their behavior, measure their
> improvement, and turn their trading history into credible evidence and
> reputation.

Short version — the entire ecosystem in four words:

> **Improve. Prove. Build Reputation. Unlock Opportunity.**

Alongside this (owner, 15 Sep 2026): KAIZEN is being built to be the best AI
for trading the way Claude is the best AI for coding — not by predicting
markets, but by knowing the trader: their rules, their record, their mind.

## 3. What KAIZEN is NOT

Not primarily: a signal provider · a strategy marketplace · a broker · an
investment manager · a prop firm · a guarantee of profitability · a platform
where buying a token buys reputation · a social-media trading leaderboard.

And for now, KAIZEN does not promise to fund traders. These may intersect the
future ecosystem; they are not the core product.

## 5. The five canonical dimensions — the ONE scoring framework

No competing scoring systems.

1. **Process** — consistency of following the trading system: pre-trade
   planning, setup qualification, entry/exit/invalidation rules, no-trade
   conditions, deviation from plan.
2. **Risk** — predefined risk, position sizing, exposure, stop/invalidation
   adherence, risk escalation, revenge-risk behavior, concentration.
3. **Execution** — planned vs actual entry/exit, execution deviation, moving
   invalidation, impulsive entries, premature exits, abandoning the plan.
4. **Behavior** — FOMO, revenge behavior, overtrading, hesitation,
   impulsivity, emotional decision-making, loss chasing, confidence
   instability.
5. **Learning & Consistency** — quality of reflection, identifying and
   correcting mistakes, repeated-mistake frequency, adaptation, consistency
   over time, improvement trajectory, response to feedback.

**Profitability is NOT one of the five dimensions.** Performance may
eventually become another evidence layer. The dimensions measure the
development process.

## 6. Score philosophy

- **Simple to understand externally** ("Process: 84, Risk: 91…"), complex and
  difficult to game internally.
- A single journal entry must never suddenly move someone 70 → 90.
- The engine considers: declared rules, trading plan, pre-trade decisions,
  trade records, post-trade reflection, actual behavior, repeated patterns,
  contradictions, historical consistency, improvement over time.
- **Core principle: KAIZEN rewards better decisions, not more activity.**
  Nobody farms XP with 500 meaningless entries.

## 7. Gamification principles

Gamification makes improvement fun — it must not turn trading into a game.

**Reward:** meaningful pre-trade plans · following rules · honestly
documenting mistakes · reviewing trades · identifying behavioral patterns ·
correcting recurring mistakes · consistency · educational challenges ·
demonstrated improvement.

**Do not reward:** "You traded 30 times today." Future badge/milestone work
follows this principle (current session-count milestones are safe — sessions
are completion-gated and cadence-guarded — but new gamification targets
meaning, not counts).

## 8. Progression identities (FUTURE)

Observer → Journaler → Process Builder → Self-Aware → Consistent →
Disciplined → KAIZEN Trader.

Progression identities, never claims of guaranteed profitability. Built after
the score engine proves itself (config/App.js B8).

## 9. The eight-stage roadmap

| Stage | Focus | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trader Improvement | **NOW — highest** |
| 2 | Evidence | **NOW — early** |
| 3 | Verification | Future |
| 4 | Credentials | Future |
| 5 | Portable Reputation | Future |
| 6 | Opportunity / Capital Discovery | Future |
| 7 | Capital Infrastructure | Far future |
| 8 | Mature KAIZEN Ecosystem | Long-term |

- **Stage 1 (now):** trading profile, trading system/rules, trading plan,
  pre-trade checklist, structured journal, post-trade reflection, KAIZEN AI
  (analyzes, explains, identifies patterns, coaches, asks questions — never
  invents the score; the deterministic engine measures, AI explains evidence).
- **Stage 2 (early):** turn the journey into structured evidence — tenure,
  rule consistency, recurring mistakes and whether they decrease, behavior
  and score change, credentials, process evolution. A trader history, not a
  pile of entries.
- **Stage 3 Verification:** "how confident are we that this evidence is
  authentic?" — external connections, verified records, cryptographic proofs
  where appropriate, immutable credential records. Blockchain serves the
  product, never the reverse.
- **Stage 4 Credentials:** meaningful ones — Process Discipline, Risk
  Management, Consistency, Improvement, Rule Adherence, Long-Term
  Development. Each states what was demonstrated and the supporting evidence.
  Never "congratulations, you're profitable" unless independently verified.
- **Stage 5 Portable Reputation:** the trader stops being an unknown person
  everywhere they go (DecentralProp lesson). Not automatic acceptance —
  credible, portable evidence.
- **Stage 6 Opportunities:** bridge between development and financial-market
  opportunities — competitions, education, communities, professional and
  capital-provider opportunities, partnerships. **Opportunities, not funding
  promises.** "More prepared, more measurable, more discoverable."
- **Stage 7 Capital Discovery:** trader → evidence → reputation → capital
  provider evaluates → appropriate arrangement. KAIZEN remains the evidence
  and reputation layer (study PROOF's model) — not necessarily the entity
  holding the money.
- **Stage 8 Capital Infrastructure:** only much later; real people's money
  means legal structure, regulatory analysis, custody, investor protection,
  risk controls, compliance, dispute handling. **Design for it. Don't build it.**

## 10. The evidence ladder

1. **Self-reported** — trader enters it. Useful, lower confidence.
2. **KAIZEN-observed** — behavior observed within KAIZEN. Stronger.
3. **Externally verified** — appropriate external trading data confirms
   activity. Stronger still.
4. **Real-capital history** — demonstrated performance managing external
   capital, where legally and appropriately available. Powerful.

External trading data (brokers/exchanges/platforms, future) exists to compare
**plan vs behavior vs outcome** — not to show P&L.

## 11. Wallets, Mantle, Zero Authority

- **Wallets:** one universal wallet interface (Stacks now; EVM and other
  networks later). **Private by default** — the user decides what becomes
  publicly verifiable. A wallet is never the reason somebody uses KAIZEN.
- **Mantle:** KAIZEN calculates; Mantle records selected credentials as
  immutable evidence ("Credential earned: Consistent Risk Management").
  Underlying data stays in KAIZEN.
- **Zero Authority:** stays optional — broader Web3 reputation complementing
  KAIZEN's trading-development reputation. Never required for core KAIZEN.

## 12. Capital economics — separated and deferred

The vault idea (investor capital → trader trades → profits split) is a
legitimate **future** architecture, but:

- **No APY/staking yield** until the underlying economics are genuine and
  legally appropriate. No artificial returns because crypto uses the word.
- If a capital marketplace ever exists, three things stay separated:
  1. Trading performance (money from actual trading)
  2. Capital/investment yield (return generated by capital itself)
  3. Platform economics (KAIZEN's infrastructure revenue)

## 13. The KAIZEN token

OPTIONAL / FUTURE. Not Stage 1, not needed for the core product.
**Token ownership must NEVER directly increase a trader's reputation score** —
reputation is earned through behavior + evidence + time + verification. Any
future utility (governance, ecosystem participation) must solve a real
ecosystem problem. We don't invent a use case today.

## 14. Leaderboard philosophy

"Highest KAIZEN score wins" is NOT the central social mechanism — it
contradicts "compete with your previous self." The primary surface is
**My Progress** (built this way). Later: **KAIZEN Challenges** — 14-Day
Process Challenge, Risk Discipline Challenge, No Rule-Breaking Challenge,
Weekly Review Challenge — competition around process, never raw score.

## 15. Trader Profile & Trader Card (FUTURE)

The public Trader Profile (score by dimension, development tenure, documented
sessions, credentials, verification level, improvement over time) becomes one
of KAIZEN's most valuable assets — and deliberately never says "KAIZEN
guarantees this trader will make money." The Trader Card (NFT) is the portable
cover of the CV, not the CV itself — build the reputation system first.

## 16. Build now / not now

**Do NOT build now:** investor marketplace · capital vaults · APY system ·
KAIZEN token · complicated blockchain infrastructure · funding marketplace ·
full reputation portability · elaborate NFT system.

**Build now:** trader profile · trading system · rules · trading plan ·
pre-trade checklist · journal · post-trade reflection · AI analysis ·
psychology · five-dimensional scoring · behavioral pattern detection ·
progress tracking · meaningful gamification · strong data architecture —
with the database structured so Stage 2 isn't painful later.

## 17. The locked technical principle

> **Today's data must be tomorrow's evidence.**

Entry, stop, target, risk, setup, reason, emotion, rule adherence, outcome,
reflection — stored structurally, never as mere text. Six months from now
KAIZEN must answer "how often did this trader violate their risk rule?" from
the data — not by asking an AI to read 600 entries and guess. That is the
difference between a journal app and a serious data platform.

## 18. Public positioning rule

The public story does not tell the eight-stage vision (no blockchain → vault
→ token → capital marketplace on the homepage). It says:

> You want to become a better trader. KAIZEN helps you build the system,
> track your decisions, understand your behavior and improve over time.
> Your progress becomes measurable evidence of your development.

The deeper vision is the company's strategic roadmap, not the homepage pitch.

## 19. The sentence that never appears

**"Become profitable" is never a promised outcome** — in positioning, roadmap
language, or credential text. Profitability is an eventual performance
outcome, not something KAIZEN can promise. KAIZEN builds the system that
improves decision-making and creates increasingly credible evidence of
improvement.

Getting Started
What is KAIZEN?

KAIZEN is an AI-powered trading discipline mentor. You plan, record, and reflect on your trading sessions, and KAIZEN measures how you actually traded against your own rules — then explains what the numbers mean. The goal is to make you more consistent, not just more informed.

Is KAIZEN a signal service or copy-trading platform?

No. KAIZEN never tells you what to trade. It analyzes how you trade — your decision-making, your rule compliance, your emotional patterns — and helps you hold yourself accountable to your own process.

How do I create an account?

Go to the Register page and sign up using your email and password, or use Google Sign-In for faster access. You will then be guided through building your first Trading System — your rules and risk numbers — which becomes the baseline KAIZEN measures against.

What information do I need to provide during onboarding?

The markets and hours you trade, your setups, one clear entry rule, and your hard risk numbers: risk per trade, maximum daily trades, and daily drawdown limit. This is saved as version 1 of your Trading System — you can refine it anytime, and every save is a new version so nothing is lost.

Using Sessions
How do I log a trading session?

Every session runs the same loop: Plan it (setup, emotional state, confidence), Record it (what actually happened, with your quick checks), then Reflect on it in your own words. KAIZEN AI responds after you reflect. The loop is the product — plan, trade, record, reflect, improve.

Do I need to specify which asset I traded?

You set the asset yourself when you record your session — no guessing from your notes. Just pick or type the pair or instrument: AUDJPY, EURUSD, BTC, Gold, anything.

How honest do I need to be in my reflections?

Completely honest — and honesty is never punished here. Writing that you felt fear or urgency but still waited for your setup is exactly the kind of disclosure that makes you sharper. Vague reflections produce vague feedback. The traders who improve fastest are the ones who write down exactly what happened, including mistakes.

Can I log backtesting sessions and analysis-only sessions?

Yes — and they are not second-class. LIVE, BACKTEST, and STUDY are first-class session types, and your patterns segment by type. One of the most valuable insights KAIZEN tracks is the gap between how you practice (backtest and study) and how you perform (live).

KAIZEN Score
How is my KAIZEN Score calculated?

The engine — not the AI — computes your score with a fixed formula from what you actually do in sessions: five dimensions (Process, Risk, Execution, Behavior, Learning) built from your plans, quick checks, reflections, and outcomes. KAIZEN AI explains what the numbers mean; it never invents them. The formula rewards consistent process — not winning trades.

Does a losing trade lower my score?

Not directly. Your score reflects whether you followed your own rules, not whether the trade was profitable. A losing trade taken in full compliance with your rules is a good process decision. A winning trade that broke your rules is dangerous — KAIZEN flags it exactly that way.

Can Psychology conversations change my KAIZEN Score?

No — never. Psychology is a private space for the mental side of trading. Your score is measured only from your sessions. Nothing you say in Psychology can raise or lower it.

What do the discipline tiers mean?

Developing (0–39%): building the foundation. Consistent (40–59%): showing improvement but inconsistencies remain. Elite (60–79%): strong process adherence. Master (80–100%): exceptional behavioral discipline. Each tier reflects your process consistency across all sessions.

Psychology
What is the difference between my sessions and Psychology?

Your sessions are where you trade and where your score comes from. Psychology is a separate, private space for talking through your mindset, fears, confidence, or pressure around trading in general — not tied to any specific trade. It never feeds your score; it exists so the mental side gets its own honest attention.

What can I talk about in a Psychology Session?

Anything affecting your trading mindset — fear of pulling the trigger on valid setups, anxiety when opening charts, pressure from needing trading income, imposter syndrome, overconfidence after winning streaks, comparing yourself to other traders, or struggling after a losing streak.

Are my psychology conversations private?

Yes, completely. Psychology conversations stay private by default. They power your conversation with KAIZEN and your private MindState overview — nothing else. They never feed your score. Refer to our Privacy Policy for full details on how this sensitive data is handled.

Do psychology conversations affect my KAIZEN Score?

No. Your score comes only from your sessions — what you planned, what you did, what you reflected on. Psychology is where you work on the mind behind the process, and that work is never graded.

On-Chain Reputation
What is the Mantle AI Benchmark?

Score changes and milestones from your KAIZEN journey are permanently recorded on the Mantle blockchain via our KaizenBenchmark smart contract. This creates a public, verifiable record of your progress that anyone can check on the Mantle explorer — your reputation becomes evidence, not claims.

What is Zero Authority DAO reputation?

Zero Authority DAO (ZA DAO) is a decentralized reputation platform built on the Stacks blockchain. Connecting your ZA account to KAIZEN displays your on-chain trading credentials — bounty completions, endorsements, and activity score — alongside your KAIZEN Score. Together they form your complete on-chain trader profile.

How do I connect my Zero Authority DAO account?

Visit zeroauthoritydao.com, create or log into your account, and find your ZA profile ID. Then contact us via X at @kaizenaibot and we will connect it to your KAIZEN account. Once connected, your ZA reputation will display automatically on your Reputation page.

Is my raw journal content ever put on a blockchain?

Never. Only a one-way cryptographic hash of your internal user ID, along with score changes and milestone labels, is ever recorded on-chain. Your actual session text, psychology conversations, and any personally identifiable information never leave KAIZEN's secure database.

Do I need a crypto wallet to use KAIZEN?

No. KAIZEN records blockchain data on your behalf using our own wallet. You never need MetaMask, MNT tokens, or any crypto to benefit from the on-chain reputation features. Everything happens automatically in the background when you log sessions.

Account Management
How do I update my Trading System?

Go to Settings and open My Trading System. Every save creates a new version, so KAIZEN can track how your system has evolved over time — your previous rules are never lost.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Password reset via email is not yet available. Please reach out to us on X at @kaizenaibot and we will help you regain access to your account directly.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Reputation page and select Delete Account under the Danger Zone section. This action is permanent and will remove your personal data — sessions, psychology conversations, and memories — in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Can I use KAIZEN on mobile?

Yes. KAIZEN is a mobile-first web application that works in any browser on any device. A dedicated native app for Android and iOS is on our roadmap.

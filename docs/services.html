What KAIZEN Gives You

Every feature is built around one principle — measurable improvement. Free to start. No subscriptions required right now.

The Improvement Engine
Your Trading System

Define your strategy once: market conditions, setups, entry and exit rules, risk rules, trading hours, and your no-trade conditions. KAIZEN measures every session against YOUR system — because it can't score adherence if it doesn't know the plan. As your strategy evolves, your system evolves with it.

AI Journal Analysis

Write freeform entries about your trading sessions, backtests, or market analysis. KAIZEN AI reads every entry against your own stated rules and full session history, then delivers specific behavioral feedback — never generic advice.

Behavioral Insights

Your patterns, detected over time: FOMO after consecutive losses, premature entries, discipline dips on Fridays. The patterns your P&L will never show — only your journal can reveal them.

Psychology Sessions

A dedicated chat space — separate from your trade journal — for working through mindset, fear, anxiety, or financial pressure with KAIZEN AI. Discipline failures are often rooted in psychology that never shows up in a trade log.

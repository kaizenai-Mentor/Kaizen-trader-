How It Works

The improvement loop

Plan→ Trade→ Reflect→ Analyze→ Improve→ Repeat
01
Plan

Define Your System

Your setups, entry and exit rules, risk rules, and no-trade conditions — set once, refined as you grow. This becomes the standard KAIZEN measures you against. Your rules, not ours.

02
Trade

Log Every Session

Plan before you enter. Journal after you exit. What you saw, what you did, what you felt. Honest notes, every time.

03
Reflect

Face What Happened

Did you follow your plan? What went wrong? What did you learn? Structured reflection is where a session becomes a lesson.

04
Analyze
改

KAIZEN AI Responds

The AI reads every entry against your rules and your full history, identifies your patterns, and gives you specific, actionable feedback. Never generic advice.

05
Improve

Watch Yourself Get Better

Your KAIZEN Score updates after every session, across five dimensions. Improvement becomes visible — and over time, provable.
